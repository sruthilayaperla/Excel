📊 Overview
This project demonstrates the use of Microsoft Excel for data analysis, business insights, and reporting.

The case study is part of an Excel analytics portfolio and focuses on transforming raw business data into meaningful information that can support better decision-making.

The project combines an Excel analysis workbook with supporting documentation to demonstrate a structured approach to working with business data.

🎯 Project Objectives
The main objectives of this case study are to:

Analyze business data using Microsoft Excel.
Organize and interpret raw data in a structured manner.
Identify important patterns and trends within the dataset.
Calculate relevant metrics to support business analysis.
Present analysis in an easy-to-understand format.
Convert data into actionable business insights.
Demonstrate practical Excel skills applicable to real-world analyst roles.

🔎 Analysis Approach
The analysis follows a structured process.

1. Understand the Data
The first step is to understand the available dataset, including the purpose of the data, relevant fields, and the type of information being analyzed.

2. Prepare the Data
The data is organized so that it can be analyzed consistently. This includes checking the structure of the dataset and ensuring that the information is suitable for calculations and reporting.

3. Perform Analysis
Excel is used to calculate and summarize information from the dataset.

The analysis focuses on identifying:

Important performance indicators
Trends and patterns
Differences between categories
Areas of strong and weak performance
Information that can support business decisions

4. Interpret the Results
The numerical results are interpreted from a business perspective rather than simply presenting calculations.

The objective is to answer questions such as:

What is happening in the business?
Where is performance strongest?
Where are potential problem areas?
What patterns can be observed?
What actions could be considered based on the analysis?

5. Communicate Insights
The final analysis is intended to make the information easier for stakeholders to understand and use for decision-making.
