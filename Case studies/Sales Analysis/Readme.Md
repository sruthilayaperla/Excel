📌 Project Overview

This project is an Excel-based Sales Analysis case study created to analyze sales performance across different months, products, and regions.

The workbook uses basic Excel analysis techniques such as formulas, Pivot Tables, and charts to turn raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The main objective is to understand which products, regions, and months are contributing the most to overall sales.

🎯 Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering the following questions:

What is the total sales generated? What is the average sales value? Which transaction has the highest sales? Which transaction has the lowest sales? Which month performed the best? Which product generated the highest sales? Which region contributed the most to sales? How can the sales performance be improved? 📁 Dataset

🔍 Key Insights

Based on the analysis:

Total sales are ₹145,000. March is the strongest month, with ₹57,000 in sales. Laptop is the top-selling product, contributing ₹90,000. South is the highest-performing region, generating ₹53,000. West is the lowest-performing region, generating ₹15,000. The highest individual transaction is ₹35,000. The lowest individual transaction is ₹15,000. Sales show an upward trend from January to March. 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the available data, the following actions could be considered:

Focus on Laptops

Since laptops account for the majority of sales, maintaining sufficient laptop inventory and continuing successful laptop promotions could help sustain revenue.

Improve West Region Performance

The West region has the lowest sales. Further investigation into customer demand, pricing, promotions, and distribution in this region could help identify opportunities for growth.

Analyze March's Success

March produced the highest monthly sales. Understanding what contributed to this increase could help replicate successful strategies in future months.

Monitor Product Performance

Mobile and Tablet sales are significantly lower than Laptop sales. Additional analysis could determine whether pricing, demand, promotions, or product availability is influencing their performance.
